## Decisions Made
- `useShallow` on `selectLevels()` output loops forever (fresh nested OTE pocket identity per call retriggers the subscription — 9/11 shell tests failed with Maximum update depth exceeded). Fix: subscribe to the stable `selectLevels` function and derive during render; the range/dol/rollover subscriptions already re-render on every input change since selectLevels inputs (candles/asOfBaku) move together in refresh. Same outcome the plan required (selector output flows into props, stub tests pin it), different subscription mechanism.
- Chart try/catch around `levelLineInputs` in both mount and update blocks: a guard throw nulls the four refs and leaves EQ/DOL untouched, so non-finite levels can never blank the chart.
